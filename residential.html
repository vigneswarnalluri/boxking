RESIDENTIAL SALES

Architectural Excellence For Your Kingdom

BoxKing Modular Solutions creates exquisite custom shipping container homes, backyard ADUs, and guest tiny cabins. Built from premium hand-picked 1-trip containers that are 100% hazard-free, our structural container solutions blend heavy steel integrity with high-end master structural carpentry to give you a home engineered to last generations.

  • Hand-Picked Royalty Quality: Guaranteed 1-trip container shells, completely clean and free of previous chemical cargo.
  • Fortified & Safe: Massive cargo steel skeletons engineered to withstand earthquakes, heavy snow, and strong winds.
  • Bespoke Carpentry: High-end interior millwork, smart home fixtures, and gourmet layouts tailored to your lifestyle.
